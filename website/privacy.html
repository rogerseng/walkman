Privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Cassety is a music player for iPhone. This page says what it does with your information. The short version: the people who make Cassety do not collect anything.

What Cassety does not do

  • It has no accounts and asks for no personal details.
  • It has no advertising and no tracking.
  • It has no analytics. Nothing about how you use it is sent to its developer.

What Cassety uses, and where it stays

Your Apple Music library

With your permission, Cassety reads your Apple Music library and searches the Apple Music catalogue so it can show and play your music. This happens through Apple's own MusicKit on your device. What you listen to is not sent to the developer of Cassety. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Your mixtapes and settings

The mixtapes you make, and your settings, are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is on, they are also stored in your own iCloud account so your other devices can use them. The developer of Cassety cannot see them. You can turn sync off in Cassety's settings.

Backup files

If you save a backup file, it goes wherever you choose to put it. Cassety does not keep a copy anywhere else.

Motion

Cassety reads how the phone is tilted, to move the reflections on the player. This is used on the device, at that moment, and is not stored or sent anywhere.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. The company that hosts it may keep ordinary server logs, such as the address a request came from, as every web host does.

Children

Cassety does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
