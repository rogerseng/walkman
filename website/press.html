In one line

Casseti turns your albums and playlists into cassettes: two sides, reels that turn, keys that clunk, and a tape you flip when side A runs out.

In a paragraph

Casseti is a music player for iPhone that behaves like a tape deck, not just looks like one. Every album and playlist becomes a cassette with a side A and a side B. The reels show how much of the side is left, the keys click under your thumb, and when the side ends the music stops until you turn the tape over. On a folding iPhone the cover screen is the player, and opening the phone turns it into a tape recorder with a speaker grille and a row of chunky keys. Lean on a reel too long and the deck eats your tape. It is made for listening, not skipping: with no queue to fiddle with, you stay with the song and hear the album through.
