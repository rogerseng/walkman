Support

Answers to common questions. If yours is not here, write to hello@casseti.com.

Getting started

Casseti says it needs Apple Music access

Tap Connect Apple Music and allow it. If you turned it down before, open the iPhone's Settings app, find Casseti, and turn on Media and Apple Music.

Does Casseti work with Spotify?

Not at the moment. Casseti plays music from Apple Music. Spotify's rules for developers limit apps that play its music in ways that do not fit a paid app like Casseti, so there is no Spotify support for now. If that changes, it will be announced here.

Nothing plays

Casseti needs an active Apple Music subscription on the Apple Account signed in on the phone. Check that the song plays in Apple's Music app. Some songs are not available in every country.

How do I choose a tape?

On the tape deck, press the key with the two music notes to open your library. Browse by playlist, artist, album or song, or search. On a folding iPhone, open the phone: the library is on the left.

How do I play one particular song?

Open an album or playlist and tap the song. The tape goes in wound on to that song. With a tape already playing on the closed phone, the list button at the top of the player shows its songs.

How do I take the tour again?

Open Settings and choose "Take the hands-on tour" under How the deck works. It is offered while the tape deck is the player showing.

Tapes and sides

The music stopped and play will not stay down

The tape has reached the end of the side. A card over the tape says so: tap "Flip" on it, or double tap the tape, and it carries on with the other side. If you would rather it turned itself over, switch on "Flip at the end of a side" in Settings.

How do I skip a song or wind the tape?

Tap fast forward or rewind to skip to the next or previous song. Hold either one to wind the tape, and let go where you want to listen.

How do I flip the tape?

Double tap the tape. If you flip part way through a side, you land as far from the end of the other side as you were from the start of this one, as with a real cassette.

The lights and speakers do not match the music

They are for show. Apple Music does not let an app listen to the music itself. They move at the song's tempo when your library knows it and in a steady beat when it does not. You can turn the lights off in Settings.

The music slurs when I press play or stop

That is the tape running up to speed and slowing down. Switch off "Tape runs up to speed and down to a stop" in Settings if you would rather it did not.

Sync and backup

My settings are not on my other device

Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account, with iCloud turned on for Casseti, and "Sync with iCloud" switched on in Casseti's settings. Sync can take a minute or two.

How do I back up and restore?

In Settings, "Save a backup file" writes your settings to a file wherever you choose. "Restore from a backup file" reads one back.

Themes and settings

How do I change the theme?

Open Settings and choose a colour at the top. Each has a small picture of the player. On the tape deck, Settings is the black key with the gear. In the library, it is the gear at the top.

How do I disconnect Apple Music?

In Casseti's Settings, under Music service, choose Disconnect. iOS only lets you withdraw access in the iPhone's own Settings app, so Casseti takes you there: switch off Media and Apple Music for Casseti.

How do I turn off the sounds or the haptics?

In Settings, under Sound and under Look and feel.

Purchases

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.
