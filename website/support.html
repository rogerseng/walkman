Support

Answers to common questions. If yours is not here, write to support@cassety.com.

Getting started

Cassety says it needs Apple Music access

Tap Connect Apple Music and allow it. If you turned it down before, open the iPhone's Settings app, find Cassety, and turn on Media and Apple Music.

Nothing plays

Cassety needs an active Apple Music subscription on the Apple Account signed in on the phone. Check that the song plays in Apple's Music app. Some songs are not available in every country.

How do I choose a tape?

On the tape player, press the key with the music note to open your tape shelf. On the MP3 player, choose Music from the main menu. On a folding iPhone, open the phone: the shelf is on the left.

Tapes and sides

The music stopped and play will not stay down

The tape has reached the end of the side. Double tap the tape to turn it over, then it carries on with the other side. If you would rather it turned itself over, switch on "Flip at the end of a side" in Settings.

How do I skip a song or wind the tape?

Tap fast forward or rewind to skip to the next or previous song. Hold either one to wind the tape, and let go where you want to listen.

How do I flip the tape?

Double tap the tape. If you flip part way through a side, you land as far from the end of the other side as you were from the start of this one, as with a real cassette.

The lights in the volume slot do not match the music

They move in a steady beat while a tape plays. They are for show. Apple Music does not let an app listen to the music itself, so they cannot follow it. You can turn them off in Settings.

Mixtapes

How do I record a mixtape?

Open the tape shelf and choose "Record a new mixtape". Name it, pick a C-60 or C-90, then search for songs and add them to side A and side B.

Why can I not add a song?

It is longer than the tape left on that side. Add it to the other side, remove a song, or use a C-90.

How do I edit or delete a mixtape?

On the tape shelf, swipe the mixtape to the left and choose Edit or Delete.

Sync and backup

My mixtapes are not on my other device

Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account, with iCloud turned on for Cassety, and "Sync with iCloud" switched on in Cassety's settings. Sync can take a minute or two.

How do I back up and restore?

In Settings, "Save a backup file" writes your mixtapes and settings to a file wherever you choose. "Restore from a backup file" reads one back. Restoring brings back the tapes in the file and keeps any you have made since.

Themes and settings

How do I change the theme?

Open Settings and choose under Theme. On the tape player, Settings is the black key with the gear. On the MP3 player, it is "Settings and Themes" in the main menu. On the tape shelf, it is the gear at the top.

How do I turn off the sounds or the haptics?

In Settings, under Sound and under Look and feel.

Purchases

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Contact

Write to support@cassety.com. It helps to say which iPhone you have and which version of iOS and of Cassety you are using. The version of Cassety is at the foot of its Settings screen.
