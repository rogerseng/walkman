# Casseti privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

The published copy is `website/privacy.html`. Keep the two the same.

Casseti is a music player. This page says what it does with your information. The short version: the people who make Casseti do not collect anything.

## What Casseti does not do

* It has no accounts and asks for no personal details.
* It has no advertising and no tracking.
* It has no analytics. Nothing about how you use it is sent to its developer.

## What Casseti uses, and where it stays

**Your Apple Music library.** With your permission, Casseti reads your Apple Music library and searches the Apple Music catalogue so it can show and play your music. This happens through Apple's own MusicKit on your device. What you listen to is not sent to the developer of Casseti. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

**Your settings.** Your settings are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is on, they are also stored in your own iCloud account so your other devices can use them. The developer of Casseti cannot see them. You can turn sync off in Casseti's settings.

**Backup files.** If you save a backup file, it goes wherever you choose to put it. Casseti does not keep a copy anywhere else.

**Motion.** Casseti reads how the phone is tilted, to move the reflections on the player. This is used on the device, at that moment, and is not stored or sent anywhere.

## Children

Casseti does not collect information from anyone, including children.
